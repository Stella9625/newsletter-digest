GDS weighs in on the NHS's decision to retreat from Open Source

GDS（英国政府数字服务）就 NHS（英国国家医疗服务体系）退出 Open Source（开源）领域的决定表态

Now the Government Digital Service have joined the conversation withAI, open code and vulnerability risk in the public sector, published May 14th. Their key recommendation:
Keep open by default. Making everything private adds additional delivery and policy costs, and can reduce reuse and scrutiny. Openness should remain the default posture, with closure used sparingly and deliberately.

英国 Government Digital Service（政府数字服务，GDS）于5月14日就 AI（人工智能）、open code（开放代码）以及 vulnerability risk（漏洞风险）等议题加入公共部门（public sector）的相关讨论。他们的核心建议如下：

默认保持开放。将所有内容私有化会增加额外的交付与政策成本，并可能降低复用性与审查力度。开放应继续作为默认姿态，而封闭则应谨慎且有意识地使用。
Keep open by default. Making everything private adds additional delivery and policy costs, and can reduce reuse and scrutiny. Openness should remain the default posture, with closure used sparingly and deliberately.

It is even rarer for those internal disagreements to spill over into public.

在英国 Civil Service（公务员体系）中，你偶尔会听到“被邀请参加一个没有饼干的会议”（being invited to a meeting without biscuits）这种说法。它暗示着一场相当冷淡的讨论，没有任何正常会议中的礼貌客套。不过一般来说，即使人们存在严重分歧，也很少会情绪失控。而这些 internal disagreements（内部分歧）公开化则更为罕见。
