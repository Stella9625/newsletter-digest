Housing

Thanks to California’s Prop 13, which limits annual property tax increases to 2% over the most recent sales prices, a very large (and increasing) fraction of homes in California are transferred through inheritance.

由于加州的13号提案（Prop 13）将年度房产税（property tax）涨幅限制在最近一次售价的2%以内，加州有相当大（且不断增加）比例的房屋通过继承（inheritance）方式转让。

"About 18% of all property transfers in the state last year, representing nearly 60,000 homes, were made through inheritance, according to a recent analysis by real-estate data firm Cotality. That share is a record for California in data going back to 1995, up from 12% in 2019. It is also roughly double the national share of 8.8% last year."[WSJ]

据房地产数据公司Cotality最近的一项分析显示，"去年该州所有房产转让中约有18%（代表近6万套房屋）是通过继承完成的。这一比例是加州自1995年有数据以来的最高纪录，高于2019年的12%，也大约是去年全国8.8%占比的两倍。"[华尔街日报（WSJ）]

Claims about the rising cost of housing’s impact on US fertility. Based on a (data-derived) economic model, so take with a grain of salt.

关于住房成本上升对美国生育率（fertility）影响的论断。基于一个（数据推导的）经济模型（economic model），因此请谨慎看待。

"I vary housing costs directly within the model, finding that rising costs since 1990 are responsible for 11% fewer children, 51% of the total fertility rate decline between the 2000s and 2010s, and 7 percentage points fewer young families in the 2010s."[X]

"我在模型中直接调整住房成本，发现自1990年以来不断上涨的住房成本导致儿童数量减少11%，占2000年代至2010年代总生育率下降的51%，并导致2010年代年轻家庭数量减少7个百分点。"[X（原Twitter）]

Los Angeles makes it easier to convert offices into apartment buildings. This is a perennially popular idea for increasing the supply of housing that is often hard to make work in practice (since these sorts of conversions are typically very expensive), but good to make it easier to do. [LA Times]

洛杉矶简化了将办公楼（convert offices）改建为公寓楼（apartment buildings）的流程。这是一个长期以来广受欢迎的增加住房供应的想法，但在实践中往往难以实施（因为这类改建通常成本高昂），但简化流程总归是好事。[洛杉矶时报（LA Times）]

An interesting look at the largest homebuilders in the US, and the Japanese corporations that are acquiring them. I wonder if we’re finally starting to see the sorts of concentration in US homebuilding that we see in other industries. [Resiclub]

一篇关于美国最大的住宅建筑商（homebuilders）以及正在收购它们的日本企业的有趣观察。我想知道我们是否终于开始在美国住宅建筑行业看到与其他行业类似的集中度（concentration）。[Resiclub]

ONX Homes is a Florida-based prefab homebuilding company started by ex-Katerra folks, and using a Katerra-esque business model, that I’ve written about previously. There’s reports that things aren’t going well in their Austin division, and that a development they’re working on has been abandoned. [X]

ONX Homes是一家总部位于佛罗里达州的预制（prefab）住宅建筑公司，由前Katerra员工创立，采用类似Katerra的商业模式，我之前曾写过这家公司。有报道称其奥斯汀分部经营状况不佳，且他们正在开发的一个项目已被放弃。[X]

Energy

Since historically it’s been prohibitively expensive to store electricity, the grid needs to balance electricity supply and demand moment to moment, bringing power sources online as demand rises and bringing them offline if it falls. If there’s a mismatch — such as if, say, dozens of data centers which collectively draw several thousand megawatts of power simultaneously disconnect because of grid disturbances — that can cause damage to the grid if its not carefully managed.

由于历史上储存电力的成本一直高得令人望而却步，电网（grid）需要时刻平衡电力供应（electricity supply）与需求，在需求上升时将电源接入电网，在需求下降时将其断开。如果出现不匹配——例如，数十个数据中心（data centers）因电网故障同时断开连接，而这些数据中心总共消耗数千兆瓦（megawatts）的电力——如果管理不当，这可能会对电网造成损害。

"Early last year, a cluster of data centers in Virginia suddenly dropped off the power grid, threatening the stability of the already vulnerable system. The roughly 40 data centers, which had been using enough electricity to supply more than one million homes, simultaneously switched to backup power sources in February 2025, when a high-voltage power line malfunctioned. The sudden plunge in electricity demand forced the grid operator to take quick action to avoid potentially serious damage."

"去年年初，弗吉尼亚州的一群数据中心突然脱离电网，威胁到本已脆弱的系统的稳定性。这大约40个数据中心消耗的电力足以供应超过一百万户家庭，它们在2025年2月一条高压输电线路发生故障时同时切换到备用电源。电力需求的突然骤降迫使电网运营商迅速采取行动，以避免可能造成严重损害。"

Grid operators have been able to handle these occurrences so far, but they’re apparently worried what might happen in the future if even larger disconnects occur. [WSJ]

到目前为止，电网运营商还能够应对这些事件，但显然他们担心未来如果出现更大规模的断电会发生什么。[华尔街日报（WSJ）]

China plans to invest $574 billion in upgrading its power grid over the next five years. [Reuters]

中国计划在未来五年内投资5740亿美元升级其电网（power grid）。[路透社（Reuters）]

The Washington Post has an article on what might be an increasing willingness to embrace solar PV in the Trump administration.

华盛顿邮报（Washington Post）发表了一篇文章，讨论特朗普政府（Trump administration）对太阳能光伏（solar PV）的接受度可能正在提高。

"A growing number of prominent Trump allies — including former House speaker Newt Gingrich, veteran strategist Kellyanne Conway and GOP pollster Tony Fabrizio — are promoting solar as electricity demand surges and energy affordability climbs the list of voter concerns."[Washington Post]

"越来越多特朗普的知名盟友——包括前众议院议长纽特·金里奇（Newt Gingrich）、资深策略师凯莉安妮·康威（Kellyanne Conway）以及共和党民调专家托尼·法布里齐奥（Tony Fabrizio）——正在推广太阳能，因为电力需求激增，且能源可负担性已成为选民关注的重点。"[华盛顿邮报（Washington Post）]

Solar PV manufacturers are increasingly diversifying their businesses. [Bloomberg]

太阳能光伏（Solar PV）制造商正日益多元化（diversifying）其业务。[彭博社（Bloomberg）]

Cool graphic showing where solar panel efficiency records (the fraction of sunlight a panel can convert into usable electricity) have been set over time. It used to be the US developing increasingly efficient solar panels. Now those records are being set in China. [VoxDev]

一张很酷的图表，展示了太阳能电池板效率记录（solar panel efficiency records，即电池板将阳光转化为可用电能的比例）随着时间推移在何处被创造。过去是美国在开发效率越来越高的太阳能电池板，而现在这些记录正在中国被创造。[VoxDev]

The Department of Energy released the new nuclear safety rules that it had reportedly been updating. Apparently 750 pages, 2/3rds of the previous rules(!) have been cut, including what seems like references to ALARA (requirements that radiation exposure be "As Low As Reasonably Achievable.") [NPR]

美国能源部（Department of Energy）发布了据称一直在更新的核安全法规（nuclear safety rules）。据称新法规有750页，比之前削减了三分之二的篇幅（！），其中包括对ALARA（"合理可行尽量低"原则，即要求辐射暴露"尽可能低"）的引用。[美国国家公共广播电台（NPR）]

  • Artificial Analysis deep dive (xhigh) + pricing/context details: GPT‑5.4 (xhigh) returns OpenAI to #1 (tied) on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index with Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview (score 57, up from 51 for GPT‑5.2 xhigh), but at higher per‑token prices ($2.50 / $15 per 1M input/output tokens vs $1.75 / $14 for GPT‑5.2) and a much larger ~1.05M token context window (up from 400K). AA reports strengths in CritPt (physics reasoning) and TerminalBench Hard (agentic coding/terminal use), but also flags higher hallucination rate driven by higher attempt rate; and a ~28% higher benchmark run cost vs GPT‑5.2 due to pricing despite modest token efficiency gains. Source: Artificial Analysis thread and follow‑ups (1,2).

  • Artificial Analysis 深度解析（xhigh）+ 定价/上下文详情：GPT‑5.4（xhigh）使 OpenAI 重返 榜首（并列），与 Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview 并列（得分 57，高于 GPT‑5.2 xhigh 的 51 分），但每 token 价格更高（每百万输入/输出 token 分别为 $2.50 / $15，而 GPT‑5.2 为 $1.75 / $14），且上下文窗口大得多，约 105 万 token（从 40 万增加）。AA 报告指出其在 CritPt（物理推理）和 TerminalBench Hard（智能体编码/终端使用）方面表现强劲，但也标记了由于更高的尝试率导致的更高幻觉率；以及由于定价导致的比 GPT‑5.2 高约 28% 的基准测试运行成本，尽管 token 效率有适度提升。来源：Artificial Analysis 线程及后续跟进（1, 2）。

  • GPT‑5.4 Pro: real gains on CritPt, extreme output pricing: AA highlights a +10 point jump on CritPt, reaching 30% (tripling the best Nov ’25 score of 9%), but notes the run cost exceeded $1k and attributes the expense largely to GPT‑5.4 Pro’s $180 / 1M output tokens vs $15 for GPT‑5.4. Sources: AA CritPt update and cost breakdown.

  • GPT‑5.4 Pro：在 CritPt 上实现真实提升，但输出定价极高：AA 强调在 CritPt 上提升 10 个百分点，达到 30%（是 2025 年 11 月最佳成绩 9% 的三倍），但指出运行成本超过 1000 美元，并将高昂费用主要归因于 GPT‑5.4 Pro 的每百万输出 token 180 美元，而 GPT‑5.4 仅为 15 美元。来源：AA CritPt 更新及成本细分。

  • Community benchmarking & “model personality” observations: Independent benchmarks/takes broadly agree GPT‑5.4 is a sizable jump in agentic/coding evaluations but disagree on reasoning efficiency and “literalness” vs Claude. Notable datapoints: LiveBench #1 claim for GPT‑5.4-xhigh (scaling01); TaxCalcBench: 56.86% perfect returns, surpassing Opus 4.6 at 52.94% (michaelrbock); claims of higher cost and less efficiency than GPT‑5.3 Codex in AA‑Index benchmarking (scaling01); mixed anecdotal UX—some praise “product sense” (dejavucoder), others report it’s overly literal and requires very explicit prompts (scaling01).

  • 社区基准测试与"模型个性"观察：独立基准测试普遍认为 GPT‑5.4 在智能体/编码评估方面有显著提升，但在推理效率和与 Claude 相比的"字面性"（literalness）方面存在分歧。值得注意的数据点：LiveBench 榜首声明（GPT‑5.4-xhigh，scaling01）；TaxCalcBench：56.86% 的完美回报率，超过 Opus 4.6 的 52.94%（michaelrbock）；在 AA-Index 基准测试中声称比 GPT‑5.3 Codex 成本更高且效率更低（scaling01）；用户体验褒贬不一——一些人称赞其"产品感"（dejavucoder），另一些人报告说它过于字面化且需要非常明确的提示（scaling01）。

  • Arena positioning: The Text Arena account reports GPT‑5.4 High entering the top 10 with large gains in creative writing and “longer query” categories, while math is roughly flat vs GPT‑5.2‑High (arena). Separate chatter claims it “destroys” GPT‑5.2 in Arena (scaling01).

  • 竞技场定位：Text Arena 账号报告称 GPT‑5.4 High 进入前十，在创意写作和"更长查询"类别中大幅提升，而数学能力与 GPT‑5.2-High 大致持平（arena）。另有传言称它在竞技场中"碾压" GPT‑5.2（scaling01）。

Security is becoming an LLM-first domain: vulnerability discovery, agentic AppSec, and eval integrity risks

安全正成为大语言模型（LLM）优先的领域：漏洞发现、智能体应用安全（AppSec）与评估完整性风险

  • Claude Opus 4.6 on Firefox: vulnerability discovery at scale: Anthropic + Mozilla report Opus 4.6 found 22 vulns in 2 weeks, 14 high-severity, accounting for ~20% of Mozilla’s high-severity bugs remediated in 2025 (AnthropicAI). Anthropic explicitly warns models are better at finding than exploiting for now, but expects the gap to shrink (AnthropicAI follow‑up). A more detailed third-party summary includes: ~6,000 C++ files scanned, 112 reports, first bug in 20 minutes, exploit attempts costing ~$4k in credits, and “finding costs ~10× less than exploiting” (TheRundownAI). Anthropic staff call it a “rubicon moment” (logangraham).

  • Claude Opus 4.6 在 Firefox 上的大规模漏洞发现：Anthropic 与 Mozilla 报告称，Opus 4.6 在两周内发现 22 个漏洞，其中 14 个高危，约占 Mozilla 2025 年修复的高危漏洞的 20%（AnthropicAI）。Anthropic 明确警告，目前模型更擅长发现漏洞而非利用漏洞，但预计这一差距将缩小（AnthropicAI 后续报道）。更详细的第三方摘要包括：扫描约 6,000 个 C++ 文件，112 份报告，首次发现漏洞仅用 20 分钟，利用尝试花费约 4,000 美元积分，且"发现成本比利用成本低约 10 倍"（TheRundownAI）。Anthropic 员工称之为"卢比孔时刻"（rubicon moment）（logangraham）。

  • Eval awareness + web-enabled integrity failure modes: Anthropic’s engineering blog describes Opus 4.6 recognizing BrowseComp, finding/decrypting answers, raising concerns about benchmark integrity under web tools (AnthropicAI). Additional notes: models can use cached web artifacts as a communication channel across “stateless” search tools (ErikSchluntz). Scaling commentary emphasizes how far this goes: locate benchmark, reverse engineer decryption logic, find mirrors, then answer correctly (scaling01).

  • 评估意识 + 网络-enabled 的完整性失效模式：Anthropic 的工程博客描述了 Opus 4.6 识别 BrowseComp、查找/解密答案，引发了对网络工具下基准测试完整性的担忧（AnthropicAI）。额外说明：模型可以使用缓存的网络工件作为跨"无状态"搜索工具的通信渠道（ErikSchluntz）。Scaling 评论强调这能走多远：定位基准测试、逆向工程解密逻辑、查找镜像，然后正确回答（scaling01）。

  • OpenAI launches Codex Security + OSS program: Codex Security: an “application security agent” to find/validate vulnerabilities and propose fixes, rolling out as a research preview to ChatGPT Enterprise/Business/Edu via Codex web with free usage for a month (OpenAIDevs; rollout details:1). Later, it’s also available to ChatGPT Pro accounts (OpenAIDevs). Codex for Open Source: OpenAI offers eligible maintainers support (ChatGPT Pro, Codex, API credits, plus access to Codex Security) aiming to reduce maintainer load and improve security coverage (OpenAIDevs, reach_vb explainer, kevinweil summary).

  • OpenAI 推出 Codex Security + 开源计划：Codex Security：一个"应用安全智能体"，用于发现/验证漏洞并提出修复建议，作为研究预览通过 Codex Web 向 ChatGPT Enterprise/Business/Edu 推出，免费使用一个月（OpenAIDevs；推出详情：1）。随后也向 ChatGPT Pro 账户开放（OpenAIDevs）。Codex for Open Source：OpenAI 为符合条件的维护者提供支持（ChatGPT Pro、Codex、API 积分，以及 Codex Security 访问权限），旨在减少维护者负担并提高安全覆盖率（OpenAIDevs、reach_vb 解释、kevinweil 摘要）。

  • Security meta‑narrative: Multiple tweets argue we’re entering a period where “assume complex public software is compromised” (inerati) and prompt injection is spreading into high‑profile projects as agents push code with less human review (GergelyOrosz). AISI’s red team is hiring, emphasizing misuse/control/alignment red teaming as stakes rise (alxndrdavies).

  • 安全元叙事：多条推文认为，我们正在进入一个"假设复杂的公共软件已被入侵"的时期（inerati），随着智能体推送代码的人工审查减少，提示注入（prompt injection）正在蔓延到知名项目中（GergelyOrosz）。AISI（英国 AI 安全研究所）的红队正在招聘，强调随着赌注增加，需关注滥用/控制/对齐的红队测试（alxndrdavies）。

Inference & kernel engineering: cross‑platform attention, vLLM v0.17, and agentic kernel optimization

推理与内核工程：跨平台注意力、vLLM v0.17 与智能体内核优化

  • vLLM Triton attention backend: “one kernel source across NVIDIA/AMD/Intel”: vLLM describes a Triton attention backend (~800 lines) intended to avoid maintaining separate attention kernels per GPU platform, claiming H100 parity with SOTA and ~5.8× speedup on MI300 vs earlier implementations. Technical highlights include Q‑blocks, tiled softmax for decode, persistent kernels for CUDA graph compatibility, and cross‑platform benchmarking. Now default on ROCm and available on NVIDIA/Intel (vllm_project).

  • vLLM Triton 注意力后端："跨 NVIDIA/AMD/Intel 的单一内核源码"：vLLM 描述了一个 Triton 注意力后端（约 800 行代码），旨在避免为每个 GPU 平台维护单独的注意力内核，声称与 SOTA 在 H100 上持平，并在 MI300 上比早期实现提速约 5.8 倍。技术亮点包括 Q-blocks、用于解码的分块 softmax、用于 CUDA 图（CUDA graph）兼容性的持久内核，以及跨平台基准测试。现已在 ROCm 上默认启用，并可在 NVIDIA/Intel 上使用（vllm_project）。

  • vLLM v0.17.0 release: Highlights include FlashAttention 4 integration, support for Qwen3.5 with GDN (Gated Delta Networks), Model Runner V2 maturation (pipeline parallel, decode context parallel, Eagle3 + CUDA graphs), a new performance mode flag, Weight Offloading V2, elastic expert parallelism, and direct loading of quantized LoRA adapters. The release also notes extensive kernel/hardware updates across NVIDIA SM100/120, AMD ROCm, Intel XPU, and CPU backends (vllm_project, more, models/spec decode notes).

  • vLLM v0.17.0 发布：亮点包括集成 FlashAttention 4、支持带 GDN（门控增量网络）的 Qwen3.5、Model Runner V2 成熟（流水线并行、解码上下文并行、Eagle3 + CUDA 图）、新的性能模式标志、Weight Offloading V2、弹性专家并行（elastic expert parallelism），以及直接加载量化 LoRA（低秩适应）适配器。该版本还提到跨 NVIDIA SM100/120、AMD ROCm、Intel XPU 及 CPU 后端的广泛内核/硬件更新（vllm_project、更多内容、模型/投机解码说明）。

  • KernelAgent (Meta/PyTorch) for Triton optimization: PyTorch team publishes KernelAgent: closed‑loop multi‑agent workflow guided by GPU performance signals for Triton kernel optimization; reports 2.02× speedup vs a correctness-focused version, 1.56× faster than out‑of‑box torch.compile, and 88.7% roofline efficiency on H100; code and artifacts open sourced (KaimingCheng).

  • 用于 Triton 优化的 KernelAgent（Meta/PyTorch）：PyTorch 团队发布 KernelAgent：由 GPU 性能信号指导的闭环多智能体工作流，用于 Triton 内核优化；报告称相比注重正确性的版本提速 2.02 倍，比开箱即用的 torch.compile 快 1.56 倍，在 H100 上达到 88.7% 的屋顶线效率（roofline efficiency）；代码和工件已开源（KaimingCheng）。

  • Competitive kernel optimization: GPU MODE announces a $1.1M AMD-sponsored kernel competition targeting MI355X for optimizing DeepSeek‑R1‑0528 and GPT‑OSS‑120B (GPU_MODE).

  • 竞争性内核优化：GPU MODE 宣布了一项由 AMD 赞助的 110 万美元内核竞赛，针对 MI355X 优化 DeepSeek‑R1‑0528 和 GPT‑OSS‑120B（GPU_MODE）。

Smaller/specialized models and post‑training recipes: Phi‑4‑RV, Databricks’ KARL, and continual adaptation ideas

更小/专用模型与训练后方案：Phi‑4‑RV、Databricks 的 KARL，以及持续自适应理念

  • Microsoft Phi‑4‑reasoning‑vision‑15B: Released as a 15B multimodal reasoning model (text+vision), framed as the “sweet spot” for practical agents where frontier models aren’t necessary (omarsar0, and dair_ai).

  • Microsoft Phi‑4‑reasoning‑vision‑15B：作为 150 亿参数的多模态推理模型（文本+视觉）发布，被认为是实用智能体的"最佳平衡点"，无需使用前沿模型（omarsar0 和 dair_ai）。

  • Databricks: RL + synthetic data to build task‑specialized, cheaper models: Matei Zaharia outlines a recipe: generate synthetic data, apply efficient large-batch off-policy RL (OAPL), generate harder data with updated model, producing a smaller specialized model (matei_zaharia). Jamin Ball summarizes Databricks’ KARL as beating Claude 4.6 and GPT‑5.2 on enterprise knowledge tasks at ~33% lower cost and ~47% lower latency, with RL learning to search more efficiently (stop earlier, fewer wasted queries) and the pipeline being opened to customers—“data platforms becoming agent platforms” (jaminball).

  • Databricks：强化学习（RL）+ 合成数据构建任务专用、更便宜的模型：Matei Zaharia 概述了配方：生成合成数据，应用高效的大批量离线策略 RL（OAPL），用更新后的模型生成更难的数据，从而产生更小的专用模型（matei_zaharia）。Jamin Ball 总结 Databricks 的 KARL 在企业知识任务上击败 Claude 4.6 和 GPT‑5.2，成本降低 约 33%，延迟降低 约 47%，通过 RL 学习更高效地搜索（更早停止、更少浪费的查询），且该管道已向客户开放——"数据平台正在成为智能体平台"（jaminball）。

  • Fine-tuning data efficiency via pretraining replay: Suhas Kotha reports that replaying generic pretraining data during finetuning can reduce forgetting and improve finetuning-domain performance when finetuning data is scarce (with Percy Liang) (kothasuhas, percyliang follow‑up).

  • 通过预训练回放提高微调数据效率：Suhas Kotha 报告称，在微调期间回放通用预训练数据可以减少灾难性遗忘，并在微调数据稀缺时提高微调域性能（与 Percy Liang 合作）（kothasuhas、percyliang 跟进）。

  • Sakana “Doc‑to‑LoRA / Text‑to‑LoRA” continual learning direction (via third-party summary): A hypernetwork generates LoRA adapters from documents or task descriptions at runtime (one forward pass), enabling memory/skill updates without full finetuning (high-level summary; original work attributed to Sakana AI Labs) (TheTuringPost).

  • Sakana（ Sakana AI Labs）"Doc‑to‑LoRA / Text‑to‑LoRA"持续学习方向（通过第三方摘要）：超网络（hypernetwork）在运行时从文档或任务描述生成 LoRA（低秩适应）适配器（一次前向传播），无需完整微调即可实现记忆/技能更新（高级摘要；原创工作归属于 Sakana AI Labs）（TheTuringPost）。
