Designing new polymers with AI

Heather's group recently used AI to design new polymers that are significantly stronger. These materials were created and tested in the lab, and the scientists who built them were surprised by the designs. The AI had figured out certain building blocks could break in a novel way. The AI discovered a purely quantum mechanical effect, and after convincing their lab collaborators to actually synthesize it, the material turned out to be four times tougher!

Heather 的团队最近利用 AI 设计出了强度显著提升的新型聚合物（polymers）。这些材料在实验室中被制备并测试，而构建它们的科学家对这些设计感到惊讶。AI 发现某些构建单元可以以全新的方式断裂。AI 发现了一种纯粹的量子力学效应（quantum mechanical effect），在说服实验室合作者实际合成该材料后，结果证明这种材料的韧性提高了四倍！

The twenty-two-atom ligand challenge

When asked about the role and need of human scientists, Heather points out that AI has a strong understanding of academic chemistry, but is still lacking intuition. Every time an LLM is updated, Heather asks it to design a ligand that contains exactly twenty-two heavy atoms. She has yet to find one that can succeed at this seemingly simple task that any expert could do in a second! Is this the chemistry counterpart to counting 'r's in strawberry?

当被问及人类科学家的角色和需求时，Heather 指出 AI 对学院派化学有着很强的理解，但仍然缺乏直觉。每当 LLM（大语言模型）更新时，Heather 都会要求它设计一个恰好包含 22 个重原子的配体（ligand）。她至今仍未找到一个能成功完成这项看似简单的任务的模型——而任何专家都能在几秒钟内完成！这算是化学版的"数一数 strawberry 里有几个 r"吗？

Materials vs biology

The two biggest domains of AI in science have been biology and materials. We asked Heather if there could be an AlphaFold moment for materials. Her answer reframes how we should think about the field:

AI 在科学领域最大的两个应用领域一直是生物学和材料学。我们问 Heather，材料科学是否也能迎来 AlphaFold 时刻。她的回答重新构建了我们应该如何看待这个领域：

  • First, the datasets in material science are woefully lacking in comparison to the bio world. The closest to ground truth in most cases are noisy DFT datasets. These are just approximations to the real world! The datasets that are accurate are all boring, as Heather quipped "We have really good datasets for really boring chemistry." Furthermore, good experimental structures are hard to come by and require interpretation. So generating generating high-quality, novel datasets at scale would really drive the field forward.

首先，与生物领域相比，材料科学的数据集（datasets）严重不足。在大多数情况下，最接近真值（ground truth）的是带有噪声的 DFT（密度泛函理论，Density Functional Theory）数据集。这些只是对真实世界的近似！准确的数据集都很无聊，正如 Heather 打趣说的那样："我们对非常无聊的化学确实有非常好的数据集。"此外，良好的实验结构难以获得，且需要解读。因此，大规模生成高质量、新颖的数据集才能真正推动该领域向前发展。

The difficulties of trusting the literature

Heather's team has spent the last few years using NLP and later LLMs to extract data from literature. Even a few thousand data points from these papers can be valuable for guiding her group's work. One surprising result: sometimes the reported values for a property (say temperature) do not match up with the graphs in the papers! So there's lots of potential in using LLMs to mine data from the literature, just do it with care.

Heather 的团队在过去几年中一直使用 NLP（自然语言处理）以及后来的 LLM 从文献中提取数据。即使从这些论文中提取几千个数据点（data points），也能为她的团队工作提供宝贵指导。一个令人惊讶的发现是：有时报告的某个属性值（比如温度）与论文中的图表并不匹配！因此，使用 LLM 从文献中挖掘数据具有巨大潜力，只是需要谨慎操作。

The role of academia in an ever-changing world

One theme that has been running through many of our conversations has been the changing role of the academic — and the scientist — in science. When startups are raising $100s of millions and hyperscalers and Big Pharma are all ramping up AI-for-science efforts, the academic researcher needs both resources and judgement about problems to chase more than ever.

贯穿我们许多对话的一个主题是学术界（academia）以及科学家在科学中角色的转变。当初创公司筹集数亿美元，超大规模云服务商（hyperscalers）和大型制药公司（Big Pharma）都在加大 AI for Science 的投入时，学术研究人员比以往任何时候都更需要资源，以及判断该追求什么问题的敏锐眼光。

Resources include data that is organized for machine learning, access to high throughput experimentation labs, and compute resources. These are all things that academics can build together. Research on Self-Improving Agents, RL Post-Training, and Benchmark Generation

  • Meta-affiliated work on self-improvement advanced beyond fixed meta-procedures:Hyperagents / DGM-Hextends the Darwin Gödel Machine idea by allowing agents to improve not only task behavior but also theprocedure that generates future improvements. The claim is that these meta-level improvements transfer across domains including coding, paper review, robotics reward design, and Olympiad grading, addressing a key limitation of prior self-improving systems that kept the self-improvement loop itself hand-authored (Jenny Zhang).

与 Meta 相关的自我改进工作超越了固定的元程序：Hyperagents / DGM-H 扩展了达尔文哥德尔机（Darwin Gödel Machine）的理念，允许智能体（agents）不仅改进任务行为，还能改进生成未来改进的程序。据称，这些元层面的改进可以跨领域迁移，包括编程、论文审稿、机器人奖励设计和奥林匹克评分，解决了先前自我改进系统的关键局限性——即自我改进循环本身需要人工编写（Jenny Zhang）。

  • Meta also presented a broader RL post-training unification story:RLLM = RL + LM-as-RMtrains a language-model reward modelon-policyfrom the policy’s own outputs, aiming to unify post-training overeasy-to-verify, hard-to-verify, and non-verifiabletasks. The notable claim is that using a generative LM reward model can improve reward quality across task classes compared with more brittle bespoke reward setups (Jase Weston).

Meta 还提出了一个更广泛的RL（Reinforcement Learning，强化学习）后训练统一框架：RLLM = RL + LM-as-RM，通过策略自身的输出on-policy（同策略）训练语言模型奖励模型，旨在统一对易验证、难验证和不可验证任务的后训练。值得注意的是，使用生成式 LM（Language Model，语言模型）奖励模型相比更脆弱的定制奖励设置，可以提高跨任务类别的奖励质量（Jase Weston）。

  • Benchmark and environment generation is scaling up fast:WebArena-Infinityclaims a dramatic reduction in browser environment construction cost—from months of grad-student labor tounder 10 hours and <$100 per environment—while producing harder, verifiable browser-use tasks where strong open-source models now scorebelow 50%despite doing much better on legacy WebArena/OSWorld. This matters because RL for agents increasingly needs automatically generated, high-authenticity environments rather than a handful of handcrafted testbeds (Shuyan Zhou).

基准测试（Benchmark）和环境生成正在快速扩展：WebArena-Infinity 声称浏览器环境构建成本大幅降低——从数月的研究生劳动减少到不到 10 小时且每个环境低于 100 美元——同时产生更难、可验证的浏览器使用任务，其中强大的开源模型现在得分低于 50%，尽管它们在传统的 WebArena/OSWorld 上表现要好得多。这很重要，因为智能体的 RL 越来越需要自动生成的高真实性环境，而不是少数手工制作的测试平台（Shuyan Zhou）。

  • Topical RL synthesis remained popular, though less novel: a high-engagement overview from The Turing Post catalogued16 RL variantsspanning RLHF, RLAIF, RLVR, process rewards, self-feedback, and critique-based methods—useful as a taxonomy, but the more technically significant tweets this cycle were abouthow RL environments and reward models are being industrialized(Turing Post RL list).

主题性 RL 综合仍然受欢迎，尽管新意较少：The Turing Post 的一篇高参与度概述列出了16 种 RL 变体，涵盖 RLHF（基于人类反馈的强化学习）、RLAIF（基于 AI 反馈的强化学习）、RLVR（基于验证器的强化学习）、过程奖励、自我反馈和基于批评的方法——作为分类法很有用，但本周期技术上更重要的推文是关于RL 环境和奖励模型如何被工业化的。

World Models, JEPA, Mechanistic Interpretability, and Emerging Training Theory

  • JEPA/world-model work had one of the stronger technical showings of the day:LeWorldModelclaims stable end-to-end JEPA trainingdirectly from pixelswith no teacher-student tricks, no EMA, and no heavy heuristics:15M params,1 GPU, and<1 second planning, with follow-on summaries emphasizing~48–50× planning speedupsand competitive performance against prior world-model baselines. This attracted attention because JEPA-style methods have often been seen as fragile or trick-heavy; these results argue for a much simpler training recipe (Lucas Maes,Randall Balestriero,RobotsDigest).

JEPA（Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture，联合嵌入预测架构）/world-model（世界模型）工作是当天最强的技术展示之一：LeWorldModel 声称实现了稳定的端到端 JEPA 训练，直接从像素（directly from pixels）学习，没有师生技巧、没有 EMA（指数移动平均）、也没有繁重的启发式方法：1500 万参数、1 个 GPU、规划时间不到 1 秒，后续总结强调约 48-50 倍的规划加速，以及与先前世界模型基线相比具有竞争力的性能。这引起了关注，因为 JEPA 风格的方法常被视为脆弱或技巧过重；这些结果支持一种更简单的训练配方（Lucas Maes、Randall Balestriero、RobotsDigest）。

  • Mechanistic interpretability continues to mature from “vibes” into reverse engineering: a thread summarizing Anthropic’s “On the Biology of a Large Language Model” framed current mech interp as uncovering circuits and internal features with a level of specificity that would have sounded implausible a decade ago, while also cautioning that traced circuits need not correspond to what the model can explicitly verbalize about its own reasoning (summary thread).

Mechanistic interpretability（机制可解释性，简称 mech interp）正从"直觉（vibes）"成熟为逆向工程：一条总结 Anthropic 的《On the Biology of a Large Language Model》（论大型语言模型的生物学）的推文认为，当前的 mech interp 正在揭示circuits（电路/回路）和内部特征，其具体程度在十年前听起来是不可信的，同时也警告说，追踪到的电路不一定与模型能够明确口头表达的关于其自身推理的内容相对应（总结线程）。

  • Training theory and optimizer scaling also got attention: Antonio Orvieto’s thread argued that optimization theory for adaptive methods explains much of knownLLM hyperparameter scalingand can suggest transfer rules without brute-force sweeps, while follow-up discussion highlighted optimizer dependence and implications for Muon-style setups (Orvieto,giffmana reaction,leloykun follow-up). This is one of the more useful undercurrents of the day: people are trying to replace empirical scaling folklore with derivations.

训练理论和优化器（optimizer）缩放也获得了关注：Antonio Orvieto 的推文认为，自适应方法的优化理论解释了已知的大部分 LLM（Large Language Model，大语言模型）超参数缩放规律，并且可以在不进行暴力搜索的情况下提出迁移规则，而后续讨论强调了优化器依赖性和对 Muon 风格设置的影响。这是当天更有用的潜流之一：人们正试图用推导取代经验性的缩放 folklore（民间传说/传统经验）。

Document Parsing, Retrieval, and Search Infrastructure Became More “Agent-Native”

  • Document parsing is becoming a serious systems layer, not a side utility: Google Devs and LlamaIndex highlighted a workflow combiningLlamaParse + Gemini 3.1 Profor extracting structured data from difficult financial PDFs, claiming roughly15% accuracy gainson brokerage statements and complex tables. Separately, LlamaIndex’s newLiteParsetargets a lighter-weight parsing path with URL and stream support and no VLM dependency, specifically pitched as something agents can call cheaply and quickly (Google Devs,Jerry Liu,LiteParse).

文档解析正在成为一个严肃的系统层，而非边缘工具：Google Devs 和 LlamaIndex 强调了一种结合 LlamaParse 和 Gemini 3.1 Pro 的工作流，用于从困难的金融 PDF 中提取结构化数据，声称在经纪商对账单和复杂表格上获得约15% 的准确率提升。另外，LlamaIndex 的新产品 LiteParse 针对更轻量级的解析路径，支持 URL 和流式传输，且不依赖 VLM（Vision Language Model，视觉语言模型），专门定位为智能体可以廉价且快速调用的工具。

  • Search/retrieval infra for coding agents improved materially: Cursor shippedInstant Grep, advertising regex search overmillions of files in milliseconds, with a technical writeup on the indexing/algorithm tradeoffs. For agentic coding this kind of primitive matters more than another tiny model gain; search latency directly shapes whether agents can iterate over large repos fast enough to be useful (Cursor announcement,blog link).

面向编码智能体的搜索/检索（retrieval）基础设施得到了实质性改进：Cursor 发布了 Instant Grep，宣称可在毫秒级对数百万文件进行正则表达式搜索，并附有关于索引/算法权衡的技术文章。对于智能体编码来说，这种原语比微小的模型增益更重要；搜索延迟直接决定了智能体是否能足够快地迭代大型仓库以发挥作用。

  • Late interaction / multi-vector retrieval is having a moment: the Weaviate/LightOn discussion argued that late interaction systems finally look practical for broader deployment, especially for code and reasoning-heavy retrieval. The core argument: token-level multi-vector representations can still be cheaper and more reusable than full cross-encoders, while materially improving recall and ranking quality for agentic workloads (Connor Shorten podcast,softwaredoug,Amélie Chatelain).

Late interaction（延迟交互）/多向量检索（multi-vector retrieval）正处于高光时刻：Weaviate/LightOn 的讨论认为，延迟交互系统终于看起来适合更广泛的部署，特别是对于代码和重度推理的检索。核心论点是：token 级别的多向量表示仍然比完整的cross-encoders（交叉编码器）更便宜且更可重用，同时能实质性地提高智能体工作负载的召回率和排序质量。
