LLM局限软件工程人的价值
David Abram指出，软件开发中最困难的部分从来不是编写代码，而是理解系统、设计架构和做出关键决策；LLM无法解决这些核心问题，因为它们缺乏上下文理解且无法承担责任或做出选择，而开发者真正的价值在于知道应该构建什么以及背后的原因。
The real work of software development, the part that makes someone valuable, is knowing what should exist in the first place, and why.
软件开发的真正工作，让人有价值的部分，是首先知道应该存在什么，以及为什么。

Quoting David Abram

引用大卫·艾布拉姆（David Abram）的观点

I have been doing this for years, and the hardest parts of the job were never about typing out code. I have always struggled most with understanding systems, debugging things that made no sense, designing architectures that wouldn't collapse under heavy load, and making decisions that would save months of pain later.

我从事这行多年，这份工作最难的部分从来都不是敲代码。我始终最纠结的是理解系统（systems）、调试（debugging）那些莫名其妙的问题、设计不会在重载下崩溃的架构（architectures），以及做出能为日后省去数月痛苦的决策。

None of these problems can be solved LLMs (Large Language Models). They can suggest code, help with boilerplate, sometimes can act as a sounding board. But they don't understand the system, they don't carry context in their "minds", and they certianly don't know why a decision is right or wrong.

这些问题没有一个能被大语言模型（LLMs）解决。它们可以提供代码建议、帮忙处理样板代码，有时还能充当共鸣板。但它们不理解系统，它们的"思维"中不携带上下文（context），当然也不知道一个决策是对是错。

And the most importantly, they don't choose. That part is still yours. The real work of software development, the part that makes someone valuable, is knowing what should exist in the first place, and why.

最重要的是，它们不会做出选择（choose）。那部分仍然是你的。软件开发（software development）真正的工作，让一个人有价值（valuable）的部分，在于首先知道应该存在什么，以及为什么。

—David Abram, The machine didn't take your craft. You gave it up.

——大卫·艾布拉姆（David Abram），《机器没有夺走你的手艺。是你放弃了它。》

Biggest takeaways:

核心要点：

  • Automation frees you to focus on higher-value activities. As Marco puts it: “I do something else while these agents are doing whatever it is I need them to do.” When scanning his daughter’s homework, he was able to help her with math problems instead of fighting with scanner software.

    自动化让你能够专注于更高价值的活动。正如 Marco 所说："当这些代理（agents）在执行我需要它们做的任何事情时，我可以去做别的事情。"在扫描女儿的作业时，他能够帮她解决数学问题，而不是与扫描软件作斗争。

Automation frees you to focus on higher-value activities. As Marco puts it: “I do something else while these agents are doing whatever it is I need them to do.” When scanning his daughter’s homework, he was able to help her with math problems instead of fighting with scanner software.

自动化让你能够专注于更高价值的活动。正如 Marco 所说："当这些代理在执行我需要它们做的任何事情时，我可以去做别的事情。"在扫描女儿的作业时，他能够帮她解决数学问题，而不是与扫描软件作斗争。

  • Hardware automation is an underappreciated AI use case. Marco demonstrates how Warp can control his scanner through NAPS2, a CLI tool for scanners. This allows him to scan documents, flip pages, and combine PDFs without ever touching the scanner software.

    硬件自动化是一个被低估的 AI 应用场景。Marco 演示了 Warp 如何通过 NAPS2（一个扫描仪的 CLI 工具）控制他的扫描仪。这让他无需接触扫描软件即可扫描文档、翻页和合并 PDF。

Hardware automation is an underappreciated AI use case. Marco demonstrates how Warp can control his scanner through NAPS2, a CLI tool for scanners. This allows him to scan documents, flip pages, and combine PDFs without ever touching the scanner software.

硬件自动化是一个被低估的 AI 应用场景。Marco 演示了 Warp 如何通过 NAPS2（一个扫描仪的 CLI（命令行界面）工具）控制他的扫描仪。这让他无需接触扫描软件即可扫描文档、翻页和合并 PDF。

  • File manipulation is a powerful AI capability. When Marco needed to compress a 1.7GB video file, he simply asked Warp to analyze why it was so large and re-encode it with FFmpeg. The AI identified the issue (excessive bitrate and resolution) and compressed it to 13MB while maintaining quality.

    文件操作是一项强大的 AI 能力。当 Marco 需要压缩一个 1.7GB 的视频文件时，他只需让 Warp 分析它为何如此之大，并使用 FFmpeg 重新编码。AI 识别出问题（过高的比特率和分辨率），并将其压缩至 13MB，同时保持了质量。

File manipulation is a powerful AI capability. When Marco needed to compress a 1.7GB video file, he simply asked Warp to analyze why it was so large and re-encode it with FFmpeg. The AI identified the issue (excessive bitrate and resolution) and compressed it to 13MB while maintaining quality.

文件操作是一项强大的 AI 能力。当 Marco 需要压缩一个 1.7GB 的视频文件时，他只需让 Warp 分析它为何如此之大，并使用 FFmpeg 重新编码。AI 识别出问题（过高的比特率和分辨率），并将其压缩至 13MB，同时保持了质量。

  • Ad hoc agents are changing how we approach automation. Marco defines these as “unnamed agents created on the fly to do something for me.” Rather than building complex, permanent workflows, these micro-agents handle specific tasks when needed and then disappear. This approach is becoming a trend across general-purpose AI tools, blurring the line between consuming and building agents.

    临时代理（Ad hoc agents）正在改变我们处理自动化的方式。Marco 将其定义为"即时创建的、为我做某事的未命名代理"。与构建复杂的永久工作流不同，这些微代理（micro-agents）在需要时处理特定任务，然后消失。这种方法正在成为通用 AI 工具中的趋势，模糊了使用代理与构建代理之间的界限。

Ad hoc agents are changing how we approach automation. Marco defines these as “unnamed agents created on the fly to do something for me.” Rather than building complex, permanent workflows, these micro-agents handle specific tasks when needed and then disappear. This approach is becoming a trend across general-purpose AI tools, blurring the line between consuming and building agents.

临时代理正在改变我们处理自动化的方式。Marco 将其定义为"即时创建的、为我做某事的未命名代理"。与构建复杂的永久工作流不同，这些微代理在需要时处理特定任务，然后消失。这种方法正在成为通用 AI 工具中的趋势，模糊了使用代理与构建代理之间的界限。

  • CLI tools with AI create better interfaces than traditional GUIs. Marco points out that designing graphical interfaces for complex systems like Azure permissions is extremely difficult. AI with access to command-line tools can abstract away these design challenges.

    结合 AI 的 CLI（命令行界面）工具比传统 GUI（图形用户界面）创造出更好的界面。Marco 指出，为 Azure 权限等复杂系统设计图形界面极其困难。能够访问命令行工具的 AI 可以抽象掉这些设计挑战。

CLI tools with AI create better interfaces than traditional GUIs. Marco points out that designing graphical interfaces for complex systems like Azure permissions is extremely difficult. AI with access to command-line tools can abstract away these design challenges.

结合 AI 的 CLI 工具比传统 GUI 创造出更好的界面。Marco 指出，为 Azure 权限等复杂系统设计图形界面极其困难。能够访问命令行工具的 AI 可以抽象掉这些设计挑战。
