Quoting Andrew Quinn
造轮子编程学习实践学习
Andrew Quinn 认为，担心自己的实现早已被更优方案取代而不敢“造轮子”是一种陷阱。他指出，在大多数领域只需重新发明少量轮子，这种带着问题的实践远比被动学习更能快速将你推向真正的技术前沿。
My central conceit is that this is a trap. You need to reinvent a couple of wheels to get to the edge of what we know about wheel-making, not a thousand wheels, and not zero; probably four or five is sufficient in most domains, maybe closer to twenty or thirty in the most epistemically rigorous and developed fields like mathematics or computer science.
我的核心观点是，这是一个陷阱。你需要重新发明几个轮子，才能触达我们所知的轮子制造的边界——不是一千个，也不是零个；在大多数领域，四五个大概就足够了；在数学或计算机科学这类认识论最严谨、最发达的学科中，也许需要二三十个。
Each wheel you reinvent, and every directed question you ask along the way, will propel you faster to the true frontier than that same amount of time spend in idle study, or even five times that amount.
你重新发明的每一个轮子，以及沿途提出的每一个有的放矢的问题，都会比将同样的时间用于空泛的学习——甚至五倍于此的时间——更快地推动你抵达真正的前沿。

Quoting Andrew Quinn

One could say in the first quarter-century of my life, that while I was always fascinated by programming, I could never overcome the guilt of not really knowing whether the tool I am building right now isn’t already superceded by some much better implementation someone else has already written 30 or 40 years ago; I could write a TSV-aware search and replace, or I could find out aboutawkand solve that entire class of problems in one fell swoop, for example. My central conceit is thatthis is a trap. Youneedto reinvent a couple of wheels to get to the edge of what we know about wheel-making, not a thousand wheels, and not zero; probably four or five is sufficient in most domains, maybe closer to twenty or thirty in the most epistemically rigorous and developed fields like mathematics or computer science. Each wheel you reinvent, and every directed question you ask along the way, will propel you faster to the true frontier than that same amount of time spend in idle study, or even five times that amount.

可以说，在我人生的前四分之一世纪里，尽管我始终对 programming（编程） 充满热忱，却始终无法摆脱这样一种愧疚感：我永远无法确定，自己此刻正在构建的工具，是否早在三四十年前就已被别人写出过更好的实现所取代；例如，我可以写一个支持 TSV（制表符分隔值） 的查找替换工具，或者我也可以去了解 awk，然后一举解决整类问题。我的核心观点是，这是一个陷阱。你需要亲自重新发明几个轮子，才能抵达当下轮子制造技术的边缘——不是一千个，也不是零个；在大多数领域，四五个大概就足够了，而在数学或 computer science（计算机科学） 这类认识论最严谨、发展最成熟的领域，可能接近二三十个。你重新发明的每一个轮子，以及沿途提出的每一个针对性问题，都会比将同样长的时间——甚至是五倍的时间——花在空泛的学习上，更能推动你快速抵达真正的前沿。

—Andrew Quinn, footnote on Replacing a 3 GB SQLite database with a 10 MB FST (finite state transducer) binary
