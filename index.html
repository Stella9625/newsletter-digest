Matt Webb 谈架构与编程
💡 洞察
Quoting Matt Webb
智能体编程氛围编程软件架构
Matt Webb指出，AI智能体虽可通过海量计算资源暴力破解编程问题，但理想的开发模式应建立在优秀架构设计与库封装之上，以实现高效、可维护的解决方案；在这种被称为“vibing”的新范式下，开发者正从关注代码细节转向更深层的架构思考。
The thing about agentic coding is that agents grind problems into dust.
代理式编程的特点在于，智能体会将问题碾为尘埃。
While I'm vibing (I call it vibing now, not coding and not vibe coding) while I'm vibing, I am looking at lines of code less than ever before, and thinking about architecture more than ever before.
当我在'vibe'时（我现在称之为'vibe'，既非编程，也非vibe编程），我看代码行数比以往任何时候都少，而思考架构比以往任何时候都多。

Quoting Matt Webb

The thing about agentic coding is that agents grind problems into dust. Give an agent a problem and a while loop and - long term - it'll solve that problem even if it means burning a trillion tokens and re-writing down to the silicon. [...]

Matt Webb 指出，agentic coding（智能体编程）的关键在于，智能体能够将问题碾碎成尘埃。给智能体一个问题和一个 while 循环——从长远来看——即使这意味着要消耗万亿级的 token 并一直重写到底层硅片，它也会解决这个问题。[...]

But we want AI agents to solve coding problems quickly and in a way that is maintainable and adaptive and composable (benefiting from improvements elsewhere), and where every addition makes the whole stack better.

但我们希望 AI agents（AI 智能体）能够快速解决编程问题，并且以可维护、可适应和可组合（composable，受益于其他地方的改进）的方式，同时每一次新增功能都能让整个技术栈变得更好。

So at the bottom is really great libraries that encapsulate hard problems, with great interfaces that make the "right" way the easy way for developers building apps with them. Architecture!

因此在底层，我们需要真正优秀的库来封装复杂难题，配以出色的interfaces（接口），让开发者在使用它们构建应用时，"正确"的方式就是简单的方式。这就是 Architecture（架构）！

While I'm vibing (I call it vibing now, not coding and not vibe coding) while I'm vibing, I am looking at lines of code less than ever before, and thinking about architecture more than ever before.

当我在 vibing（我现在称之为 vibing，而非 coding 或 vibe coding）时，我比以往任何时候都更少关注代码行，而比以往任何时候都更多地思考 architecture（架构）。

—Matt Webb, An appreciation for (technical) architecture

——Matt Webb，《对（技术）架构的欣赏》

War in Iran

伊朗战争

The disruption to oil and LNG supplies caused by the closure of the Strait of Hormuz is causing some countries to burn more coal.
"India is burning more coal to meet higher summer demand. South Korea has lifted caps on electricity from coal. Indonesia is prioritizing using its domestic supply. Thailand, the Philippines and Vietnam are boosting coal-fired power."
[NPR]

霍尔木兹海峡（Strait of Hormuz）关闭导致的石油和液化天然气（LNG）供应中断，正促使一些国家增加煤炭消耗。

"印度正在燃烧更多煤炭以满足更高的夏季需求。韩国已取消煤电（coal-fired power）上限。印度尼西亚正优先使用国内供应。泰国、菲律宾和越南正在增加燃煤发电。"
[NPR]

And because petroleum is used to make plastic, the closure of the strait is also driving up plastic prices. Dow Chemical plans a 30 cents per pound increase in April, following a 10 centers per pound increase in March. [WSJ] Plastic prices are up nearly 40% since February. [Reuters]

由于石油（petroleum）被用于制造塑料，海峡关闭也在推高塑料价格（plastic prices）。陶氏化学（Dow Chemical）计划在4月份每磅涨价30美分，此前3月份已每磅涨价10美分。[WSJ] 自2月以来，塑料价格已上涨近40%。[Reuters]

Petroleum is also used in the manufacture of pharmaceuticals, so the strait closure might affect the supply of generic medications. [CNBC] And last week I noted that production of helium (which is extracted as part of the natural gas drilling process) had also been disrupted, but I hadn't clocked that liquified helium is used as a coolant for MRI machines. [X]

石油还用于药品（pharmaceuticals）制造，因此海峡关闭可能会影响仿制药（generic medications）的供应。[CNBC] 上周我提到氦气生产（作为天然气钻探过程的副产品提取）也受到了干扰，但我当时没有注意到液化氦气被用作核磁共振成像（MRI）机器的冷却剂。[X]

Another AWS data center in the mideast has apparently been damaged by an Iranian drone attack. [Al Jazeera]

中东地区的另一座AWS（Amazon Web Services）数据中心显然在伊朗无人机袭击（drone attack）中受损。[Al Jazeera]

To try and address rising gas prices, the EPA is temporarily waiving regulations on the sale of ethanol blended gas (which is restricted in certain locations at certain times of the year to reduce air pollution). [CNBC]

为了应对汽油价格上涨，美国环境保护署（EPA）暂时豁免了关于乙醇混合汽油（ethanol blended gas）销售的法规（该法规在一年中的特定时间、特定地点限制销售以减少空气污染）。[CNBC]

Iran has established a "safe shipping corridor" through the strait, allowing ships to pass for a $2m fee. [Lloyd's List]

伊朗已在该海峡建立了一条"安全航运走廊"（safe shipping corridor），允许船舶通过，收费200万美元。[Lloyd's List]

The closure of the strait seems to be good for Chinese battery manufacturers though: since the start of the war their stocks have spiked. "CATL's China-traded shares have risen 19 per cent; Sungrow is up 19.4 per cent; and BYD, also the world's top EV maker, has gained 21.9 per cent since the US-Israeli strikes were launched at the end of February."[FT]

然而，海峡关闭似乎对中国电池制造商有利：自战争开始以来，它们的股价飙升。"自2月底美以发动袭击以来，宁德时代（CATL）的中国交易股票上涨了19%；阳光电源（Sungrow）上涨了19.4%；全球最大电动汽车制造商（EV maker）比亚迪（BYD）上涨了21.9%。" [FT]

Housing

住房

The New York Times on theROAD to Housing Act, and the provision that would prevent the construction of build-to-rent single family homes.
"Now, with the legislation back in the House awaiting a vote, critics are urging lawmakers to drop that build-to-rent restriction, arguing it would counter the intent of the bill, making it harder, not easier, to build homes when the country desperately needs them."
[NYT] Senator Elizabeth Warren is apparently unhappy about the resistance to these provisions of the bill, and is sending vaguely threatening letters to investors in multifamily apartments and manufactured homes. [Axios]

《纽约时报》报道了《theROAD住房法案》（theROAD to Housing Act）以及其中一项将阻止建造单户出租住宅（build-to-rent single family homes）的条款。

"现在，该法案已回到众议院等待投票，批评者敦促立法者取消这项出租限制，认为这将违背法案的初衷，在国家急需住房的时候，使建房变得更难而非更容易。"
[NYT] 参议员伊丽莎白·沃伦（Elizabeth Warren）显然对针对这些条款的抵制感到不满，正在向多户型公寓和预制房屋（manufactured homes）的投资者发出含糊其辞的警告信。[Axios]

Another NYT article on the surprising success of some US malls. Apparently malls with higher-end, luxury tenants are doing fairly well.
"There are roughly 900 malls in the United States, but only a small sliver are successful. The top 100 account for 50 percent of the entire sector's value, according to Tibone, whereas the bottom 350 make up 10 percent. Revenue at class A malls is growing by 5 percent each year, and financing is easy to come by."
This is not totally surprising to me — malls in the Atlanta metro area seem crowded whenever I drive by or go in one, so I knew some must be succeeding — but it's interesting to see that it seems to be specifically the high-end malls. [NYT]

另一篇《纽约时报》文章讨论了一些美国购物中心（malls）令人惊讶的成功。显然，拥有高端奢侈品牌租户的购物中心表现相当不错。

"美国约有900家购物中心，但只有一小部分是成功的。据Tibone称，前100家占整个行业价值的50%，而垫底的350家仅占10%。A级购物中心（class A malls）的收入每年增长5%，且融资很容易获得。"
这对我来说并不完全意外——每当我驾车经过或进入亚特兰大都会区的购物中心时，它们看起来都很拥挤，所以我知道其中一些一定很成功——但有趣的是，似乎 specifically 是高端（high-end）购物中心表现突出。[NYT]

Fannie Mae will now allow mortgages backed with cryptocurrency.
"The mortgage company Better Home & Finance and the U.S. crypto exchange Coinbase Global unveiled a new mortgage product Thursday that allows home buyers to pledge their crypto holdings when getting a Fannie-backed mortgage, instead of selling the crypto to make a cash down payment."
What could go wrong. [WSJ]

房利美（Fannie Mae）现在将允许以加密货币（cryptocurrency）为抵押的抵押贷款。

"抵押贷款公司Better Home & Finance和美国加密货币交易所Coinbase Global周四推出了一款新的抵押贷款产品，允许购房者在获得房利美支持的抵押贷款时抵押其加密货币持仓，而无需出售加密货币来支付现金首付。"
这能出什么问题呢。[WSJ]

Manufacturing

制造业

Elon Musk announces "Terafab" semiconductor fab to make chips for Tesla cars, Optimus, and space-based data centers.
"We either build the Terafab or we don't have the chips," he said while on stage at an event in Austin. "We need the chips, so we're going to build the Terafab."
[WSJ]

埃隆·马斯克（Elon Musk）宣布建设"Terafab"半导体工厂（semiconductor fab），为特斯拉汽车、Optimus人形机器人和太空数据中心制造芯片。

"我们要么建造Terafab，要么就没有芯片，"他在奥斯汀一场活动的舞台上表示。"我们需要芯片，所以我们要建造Terafab。"
[WSJ]
