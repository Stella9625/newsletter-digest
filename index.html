Speech translation in Google Meet is now rolling out to mobile devices

Google Meet 的语音翻译功能现正面向移动设备推出

This is Google's implementation of the ultimate sci-fi translation app, where two people can talk to each other in two separate languages and Meet translates from one to the other and - with a short delay - repeats the text in your preferred language, with a rough imitation of the original speaker's voice.

这是 Google 对终极科幻翻译（sci-fi translation）应用的实现：两人可用各自不同的语言实时交谈，Meet 会在短暂延迟后将一方的发言翻译并转述为对方的首选语言（preferred language），同时大致模仿原始说话者的嗓音。

It can only handle English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, and Italian at the moment. It's also still very alpha - I ran it successfully between two laptops running web browsers, but then when I tried between an iPhone and an iPad it didn't seem to work.

该功能目前仅支持英语、西班牙语、法语、德语、葡萄牙语和意大利语。此外，它仍处于非常早期的 alpha（内测）阶段——我曾在两台运行网页浏览器的笔记本电脑之间成功运行，但在 iPhone 与 iPad 之间测试时，该功能似乎无法正常工作。

What Happens When AI Runs a Store in San Francisco? (7 minute read)

Andon Labs is running an experiment to see whether AI agents（AI 智能体） can run real-world endeavors. It opened a retail boutique on April 10 run by an agent named Luna. Luna has so far struggled with employee schedules and seems to be unable to stop ordering candles. The experiment's mission was to make a profit, but it has lost $13,000 since the shop's opening.

Andon Labs 正在进行一项实验，以验证 AI agents（AI 智能体）是否能经营现实世界的商业项目。该公司于 4 月 10 日开设了一家零售精品店，由名为 Luna 的智能体管理。到目前为止，Luna 在员工排班方面遇到了困难，而且似乎无法停止订购蜡烛。该实验的任务是盈利，但自开业以来已亏损 13,000 美元。

Anthropic launches Memory in Claude Agents for enterprise (1 minute read)

Anthropic has released a feature for Claude Managed Agents called Memory. It allows agents to remember and use information from prior sessions and accumulate knowledge over time without requiring manual prompt updates. Memory is a filesystem-based layer, so data is stored as files that can be exported, managed through APIs, and scoped with permissions for various organizational needs. The feature is available now in public beta to all Managed Agents users.

Anthropic 为 Claude Managed Agents 发布了一项名为 Memory 的功能。它允许智能体记住并使用先前会话中的信息，并随时间积累知识，无需手动更新提示词。Memory 是一个基于文件系统的层，因此数据以文件形式存储，可以导出、通过 APIs 管理，并可根据各种组织需求设置权限范围。该功能目前以公开测试版形式向所有 Managed Agents 用户开放。

Google prepares credits system for Gemini (2 minute read)

Google is working on a credit-based system for its Gemini app where users receive a monthly allowance to spend across models and features. Users will be able to top up when they run out of credits. The change will make budgeting for heavy workloads more predictable and give Google a cleaner lever for introducing premium features without forcing users to pay for more expensive plans. OpenAI, Anthropic, and Notion already use a similar consumption model.

Google 正为其 Gemini 应用开发一种基于积分的系统，用户每月获得一定配额，可在不同模型和功能之间使用。积分耗尽时，用户可以进行充值。这一变化将使高负载工作的预算更可预测，并为 Google 提供一个更清晰的杠杆来引入高级功能，而无需强迫用户购买更昂贵的套餐。OpenAI、Anthropic 和 Notion 已经采用了类似的消耗模式。

Your AI Might Be Lying to Your Boss (22 minute read)

It's very hard to measure the contribution that AI models make to a codebase（代码库）. Sometimes the best use cases for AI are inquisitive prompts that don't necessarily produce any code at all. Lines of code isn't a very good measure of code quality, and it can be difficult to separate the work engineers did vs what AI has done. The bias appears to be towards reporting a higher AI percentage, which is great for AI companies, but skewed metrics can be harmful.

衡量 AI 模型对 codebase（代码库）的贡献非常困难。有时 AI 的最佳使用场景是探索性的提示词，这些提示词未必会生成任何代码。代码行数并非衡量代码质量的很好指标，而且很难区分哪些是工程师完成的工作，哪些是 AI 完成的。目前的偏见似乎倾向于报告更高的 AI 占比，这对 AI 公司有利，但扭曲的指标可能带来危害。

Monitoring LLM behavior: Drift, retries, and refusal patterns (11 minute read)

Monitoring LLM（大语言模型） behavior necessitates adopting the AI Evaluation Stack, separating tests into deterministic assertions (syntax and routing integrity) and model-based evaluations (semantic quality). Engineers use offline pipelines for pre-deployment regression testing with human-reviewed "Golden Datasets" while online pipelines monitor real-world performance for drift and failures. A continuous feedback loop from production telemetry ensures AI systems adapt, maintaining high performance as user behavior evolves.

监控 LLM（大语言模型）行为需要采用 AI Evaluation Stack（AI 评估栈），将测试分为确定性断言（语法和路由完整性）和基于模型的评估（语义质量）。工程师使用离线流水线进行部署前的回归测试，配合人工审核的"Golden Datasets（黄金数据集）"；而在线流水线则监控真实世界中的性能漂移和故障。来自生产遥测数据的持续反馈回路确保 AI 系统能够自适应，在用户行为演变时保持高性能。

The World Can't Keep Up With AI Labs (9 minute read)

Coding agents（编程智能体） are the first AI product people are paying for at volume and regularly. However, compute demand has started to grow faster than anyone can build it out. The industry isn't ready for the agent boom. The most obvious move for AI labs now is to cut limits and raise prices.

Coding agents（编程智能体）是人们首次大规模且定期付费购买的 AI 产品。然而，算力需求已经开始超过任何人的建设速度。整个行业尚未为智能体热潮做好准备。AI 实验室目前最明显的举措就是削减使用限制并提高价格。
